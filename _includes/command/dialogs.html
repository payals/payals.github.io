    The prompt understands

    talks [topic]
    Open talks, optionally filtered: ai, postgres, security, platform, reliability, other
    writing [topic]
    Open writing, optionally filtered
    cv, now
    Open the CV or what I'm doing now
    open <slug>
    Open one talk or post, for example open securing-your-data
    theme light|dark|system
    Change the theme
    help, ls, clear
    List commands, list sections, clear the output
    {%- endif %}

    Turn these off if they clash with a screen reader or voice control. They never fire while you are typing in a field. Ctrl K still opens search.
