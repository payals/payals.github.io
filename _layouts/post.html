Header (title, subtitle, a meta row of separate spans: date, minutes, topic, series part), "The short version" (a findings list from tldr: front matter, 2 to 4 plain strings, escaped on output), a contents list built from the post's own h2s when it has four or more (a sticky rail from 1280px, a closed
below), the body in .prose, then the pager. The body is the post's rendered content, untouched except for presentation hooks: wide tables get a scroll wrapper, all-emphasis paragraphs get a class, and every kramdown h2 (

) gets an empty "#" link to itself whose glyph is CSS, so the text of the article is exactly the post's text. The heading text is wrapped in a span the h2 is aria-labelledby, so the heading's accessible name stays its own text, and the link is named after its section. The footer's series card gets its id="series" focus target here, not in the include, so the feeds carry plain markup.
